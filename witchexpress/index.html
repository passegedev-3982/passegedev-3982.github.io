Frequently asked questions

The game won't start or keeps crashing.

In your Steam Library, right-click Witch Express, open Properties, then Installed Files, and choose "Verify integrity of game files". Updating your graphics driver also fixes many crashes. If it still happens, send us the form above with your log file.

Where is the log file?

Open this folder in File Explorer and attach Player.log (Player-prev.log is the run before):
%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\G1C\Witch Express

Where are my save files?

In the same folder as the log file:
%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\G1C\Witch Express

Which languages are supported?

English, Korean and Japanese.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Steam. Request one from Steam Support: help.steampowered.com

When does the game come out?

The release date is not set yet. Add Witch Express to your Steam wishlist and Steam will email you on launch day.
